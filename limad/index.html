Sovereign Wealth · Reward01

Head of Total Reward and Performance for a Sovereign Wealth Fund

Middle East Head of Total Reward and Performance

Context

We partnered with a Middle East based sovereign wealth fund to hire its Head of Total Reward and Performance. This was a critical and confidential replacement, as the highly regarded incumbent had been with the organisation for five years. We met the Chief People Officer in person to take the full brief.

Methodology

The role called for a background in investment banking or asset management, the ability to work with senior leadership and deputise for the Chief People Officer, and the credibility to advise portfolio companies. We ran a global search across the major financial centres, including London, Singapore and New York, drawing on our network and our research capability. Each candidate was assessed on technical knowledge, motivation and appetite to relocate.

Results

We sourced a candidate based in Australia who had previously held a Regional Head of Reward role in investment banking in Singapore.

Banking · HR Transformation02

Building a Strategic HR Leadership Team for a UAE Bank's Transformation

Abu Dhabi SVP HR Directors, Strategic HRBPs

Context

An Abu Dhabi headquartered bank asked us to hire a number of SVP level HR Business Partners as part of a transformation of its People function, following the appointment of a new Global Head of HR Business Partnering. The roles supported both infrastructure and revenue generating client groups, and needed strong leaders with C suite partnering experience, the ability to connect easily with people and a track record of supporting a business through growth and change.

Methodology

We targeted candidates from banking and retail and consumer organisations, researching talent at retail banks in Europe and Singapore that had undergone a similar transformation. We also searched for talent already based in the region within international banks and retail organisations.

Results

We hired two SVP level HR Business Partners, one from a retail organisation in Dubai and one from a retail bank in Italy.

Technology · Private Equity03

CHRO Appointment for a Private Equity Backed Technology Business

Munich CHRO

Context

A global technology business was undergoing a significant organisational separation and transformation following private equity investment. As it became a standalone organisation, it needed a CHRO to shape and deliver the people strategy, lead cultural change, build scalable HR infrastructure and manage complex stakeholder relationships across multiple regions.

Methodology

We ran a targeted international search, focusing on senior HR leaders with experience in transformation, organisational development, talent strategy and change management within complex multinational environments. Our assessment prioritised candidates who had led large scale cultural change, built HR functions in evolving organisations, partnered with executive leadership teams and operated across highly regulated international markets.

Results

The successful candidate brought more than 20 years of experience leading people, talent, organisational development and transformation programmes across Europe, Asia and North America, and was ideally positioned to support the organisation through its next phase of growth and transformation.

Consumer · Organisation Development04

Enhancing Organisational Effectiveness for a Global Consumer Brand

London and Paris Head of OD and Change, Head of Capability

Context

The recruitment of two specialist roles, Head of Organisation Design and Change Management and Head of Organisation and Capability Development, for a global consumer brand implementing a new HR operating model.

Methodology

We conducted a targeted search, focusing on candidates who had created new functions within global, matrixed environments. The search emphasised change management expertise and the ability to navigate complex HR challenges at pace, identifying candidates from organisations that had undergone significant transformation.

Results

Two highly experienced professionals were appointed. The Head of Organisation Design and Change Management brought a Big 4 consultancy background and experience of large scale business transformation in retail. The Head of Organisation and Capability Development brought experience of leading organisation development following a merger.

Technology · Rapid Growth05

Addressing HR Challenges in a Rapidly Growing Global Technology Firm

London and Amsterdam HR Director EMEA

Context

The recruitment of an HR Director EMEA for a global technology firm experiencing rapid growth. The firm needed an urgent replacement to support its expanding organisation and navigate the HR challenges that came with it.

Methodology

We ran a tailored search targeting highly commercial, global HR professionals able to thrive in ambiguous environments. Candidates were sourced from a broad range of sectors, including FMCG, technology, pharmaceuticals, management consultancy and investment banking, and each was assessed against specific criteria within a four week timeframe.

Results

The appointed candidate, an experienced international HR Director with a background in banking, pharma and telecoms, brought the expertise to address the challenges of rapid growth and support the organisation's continued success.

Shipping · PLC Reward06

Navigating Transformation and Executive Remuneration for a UK Shipping Firm

UK Wide Head of Reward

Context

The recruitment of a Head of Reward for a UK headquartered shipping PLC undergoing transformation and seeking to implement a comprehensive reward strategy, including executive remuneration.

Methodology

We conducted a targeted search, focusing on reward professionals with a strong track record of designing and delivering strategic reward frameworks within complex organisations undergoing transformation. The search emphasised an understanding of reward in a PLC with a varied workforce, and expertise in executive remuneration.

Results

The appointed Head of Reward, a seasoned professional experienced in developing tailored compensation and benefits packages, designed and implemented an innovative reward strategy aligned with the firm's business objectives. This improved talent attraction and retention and enhanced employee engagement, particularly at executive level.

New York and London Global Head of Executive Development
