Middle East

Sovereign Wealth · Reward01

Head of Total Reward and Performance for a Sovereign Wealth Fund

Middle East Head of Total Reward and Performance

Context

We partnered with a Middle East based sovereign wealth fund to hire its Head of Total Reward and Performance. This was a critical and confidential replacement, as the highly regarded incumbent had been with the organisation for five years. We met the Chief People Officer in person to take the full brief.

Methodology

The role called for a background in investment banking or asset management, the ability to work with senior leadership and deputise for the Chief People Officer, and the credibility to advise portfolio companies. We ran a global search across the major financial centres, including London, Singapore and New York, drawing on our network and our research capability. Each candidate was assessed on technical knowledge, motivation and appetite to relocate.

Results

We sourced a candidate based in Australia who had previously held a Regional Head of Reward role in investment banking in Singapore.

Banking · HR Transformation02

Building a Strategic HR Leadership Team for a UAE Bank's Transformation

Abu Dhabi SVP HR Directors, Strategic HRBPs

Context

An Abu Dhabi headquartered bank asked us to hire a number of SVP level HR Business Partners as part of a transformation of its People function, following the appointment of a new Global Head of HR Business Partnering. The roles supported both infrastructure and revenue generating client groups, and needed strong leaders with C suite partnering experience, the ability to connect easily with people and a track record of supporting a business through growth and change.

Methodology

We targeted candidates from banking and retail and consumer organisations, researching talent at retail banks in Europe and Singapore that had undergone a similar transformation. We also searched for talent already based in the region within international banks and retail organisations.

Results

We hired two SVP level HR Business Partners, one from a retail organisation in Dubai and one from a retail bank in Italy.

Technology · Private Equity03

CHRO Appointment for a Private Equity Backed Technology Business

Munich CHRO

Context

A global technology business was undergoing a significant organisational separation and transformation following private equity investment. As it became a standalone organisation, it needed a CHRO to shape and deliver the people strategy, lead cultural change, build scalable HR infrastructure and manage complex stakeholder relationships across multiple regions.

Methodology

We ran a targeted international search, focusing on senior HR leaders with experience in transformation, organisational development, talent strategy and change management within complex multinational environments. Our assessment prioritised candidates who had led large scale cultural change, built HR functions in evolving organisations, partnered with executive leadership teams and operated across highly regulated international markets.

Results

The successful candidate brought more than 20 years of experience leading people, talent, organisational development and transformation programmes across Europe, Asia and North America, and was ideally positioned to support the organisation through its next phase of growth and transformation.

Consumer · Organisation Development04

London and Paris Head of OD and Change, Head of Capability

Context

The recruitment of two specialist roles, Head of Organisation Design and Change Management and Head of Organisation and Capability Development, for a global consumer brand implementing a new HR operating model.

Technology · Rapid Growth05

Addressing HR Challenges in a Rapidly Growing Global Technology Firm

London and Amsterdam HR Director EMEA

Context

The recruitment of an HR Director EMEA for a global technology firm experiencing rapid growth. The firm needed an urgent replacement to support its expanding organisation and navigate the HR challenges that came with it.

Methodology

We ran a tailored search targeting highly commercial, global HR professionals able to thrive in ambiguous environments. Candidates were sourced from a broad range of sectors, including FMCG, technology, pharmaceuticals, management consultancy and investment banking, and each was assessed against specific criteria within a four week timeframe.

Results

Fintech · Reward06

Reward Director Appointment for a Global Fintech

London and Stockholm Reward Director

Context

A leading global fintech organisation needed a Reward Lead to build its international reward agenda during a period of rapid growth. Operating across multiple markets, it required a reward professional able to balance strategic design with hands on execution in a fast paced, technology driven environment, with a focus on executive compensation, equity programmes, governance and scalability, plus public company readiness.

Methodology

We focused on senior reward leaders from technology, fintech and high growth environments, with particular emphasis on executive remuneration, equity and long term incentive plans, global job architecture and reward transformation. Candidates were assessed on technical capability and their ability to influence senior stakeholders, lead teams and build reward functions in ambiguous environments.

Results

The successful candidate brought reward experience from both consultancy and in house roles, spanning technology, professional services, financial services and high growth organisations across Europe, North America and Asia Pacific.

Banking · Change and Transformation07

The Change Catalyst: A Strategic HR Director for a European Bank

London HR Director

Context

We were engaged by a global bank with a significant presence in the Netherlands, which had recently appointed a new CEO to lead its UK operations as part of a wider strategic restructure and expansion of its UK offering. Although an HR Director with more than a decade of service was in place, the bank determined that a different skillset was needed to support the evolving business and change agenda.

Methodology

We drew up an initial longlist of candidates with relevant sector experience, using both our HR network and extensive research. This was refined to a shortlist of 15, each fully interviewed by us against specific criteria within a four week timeframe. Five candidates met the executive hiring team in the Netherlands at first stage, and two progressed to final stages with the CEO and his leadership team.

Results

The appointed candidate brought extensive HR leadership experience across trading, banking and insurance, with a strong track record in change and transformation.

UK Wide Head of Reward

Motorsport · Private Equity09

CPO Appointment for a Private Equity Backed Motorsport and Engineering Business

UK Chief People Officer

Context

A private equity backed engineering organisation partnered with us to appoint its first CPO, to support a period of transformation and growth. The business needed a senior people leader able to strengthen organisational capability, embed a high performance culture and partner with the leadership team on strategic workforce priorities.

Methodology

We ran a search focused on HR leaders with experience in complex, international engineering and industrial environments, assessing each candidate's ability to lead change, influence senior stakeholders and align people strategy with business objectives. Results

Brokerage · Compensation11

US Compensation Lead Appointment for a Global Brokerage

New York US Compensation Lead

Context

A global brokerage sought a US Compensation Lead to modernise its compensation framework across a large, complex employee population, covering strategy, market benchmarking, incentive design, governance and advising senior leadership on reward.

Methodology

We focused on compensation leaders from complex, matrixed organisations with expertise in broad based compensation, salary structures, incentive programmes, market pricing and governance, and the ability to influence senior stakeholders. The package was not comparable to sector benchmarks, which made engaging candidates a key challenge, so we spoke to more than 200 profiles and mapped across industries to broaden the talent pool.

Results

The firm appointed an experienced compensation leader with a strong track record in global organisations and deep expertise in investment banking compensation strategy, incentive design, market benchmarking, executive compensation and governance, ready to support the brokerage's continued growth.
