Capability statement

Technology, automation and AI at Oakleaf Partnership

How we use technology to improve recruitment outcomes, provide market insight and help our clients reach scarce HR talent.

Oakleaf Partnership has specialised in HR, Reward and Payroll recruitment for over 21 years. Over the last two years we have invested in a technology platform of our own, built around our CRM and our website, so that our consultants see the market earlier, work with better data and spend more of their time with clients and candidates. We believe a recruitment partner should bring its own technology edge, not only a database and a job board. This statement sets out what we have built, what we have automated, and how AI supports our work.

Our core recruitment platform

  • One record of every relationship. Our CRM and applicant tracking platform holds every client, candidate, vacancy and application in one place, with relationship history that in many cases goes back to our founding in 2005.
  • Publish once, distribute everywhere. Every role is published once and distributed automatically to the job boards, to LinkedIn and to our own website, so there is one source of truth for every advert.
  • Sourcing at scale. Professional network search across the whole consultant team, with our roles and market insight amplified through each consultant's own profile.

Our website as a recruitment tool

We rebuilt oakleafpartnership.com in 2026 as part of our recruitment process rather than a brochure.

  • Live roles feed directly from our advert distribution system, with structured job data so they appear in Google for Jobs and carry branded preview cards when shared.
  • Candidate self tagging. When a candidate applies or uploads a CV they classify their own sector, level, salary band and location against the same taxonomy as our CRM, so matching starts from accurate data.
  • A consultant authorisation gate. Every website application is reviewed by the consultant who owns the role before it enters the CRM, so our client shortlists are built from vetted candidates, not raw inbound.
  • Consultant profiles showing each person's live roles, and talent community sign up for candidates who want to hear from us about their market.

Market insight

Our market intelligence comes from a proprietary platform we designed and built in house. Rather than buying a market report, we generate our own, every day.

  • A daily sweep of the HR hiring market. Our platform reads HR job adverts across the market every day and identifies who is hiring, for which roles, and at what published salary, across the UK and the US. Each lead is routed automatically to the consultant who knows that client best.
  • The HR Market Pulse. A monthly report on hiring volumes, salary ranges and sector movement in HR, built from our own daily market data alongside published labour market sources. Editions are published on our News and Views page.
  • Daily digests and watchlist alerts. Consultants receive an automated daily view of new HR hiring, with alerts when companies on a client's competitor or peer watchlist start recruiting.
  • Company verification. Client records are checked against Companies House in the UK and SEC and Department of Labor filings in the US.

For a client this means we can tell you, with evidence, what the market is paying for a role, who else is hiring for it right now, and how long comparable roles are taking to fill.

Reaching scarce talent

  • Talent communities and campaigns. We segment our candidate base by market and run targeted campaigns from CRM data, so a hard to fill role reaches the right people directly rather than relying on advert response alone.
  • Branded advertising for every role. Every vacancy is published with its own branded graphic for social channels, generated automatically, so our clients' roles stand out in a crowded feed.
  • Amplified reach. Roles reach the market through automated advert distribution, professional network search and each consultant's own network, as well as our website and talent communities.
  • A smoother candidate experience. We are piloting self service interview booking, so candidates choose a time with their consultant without a chain of emails, which shortens time to interview for in demand people.

How we use AI, and how we govern it

AI supports our consultants rather than replacing their judgment. We use leading large language models to generate market narratives, classify data and support our content and development, under one clear principle: AI prepares, people decide. Every decision that affects a candidate or a client is made by a person: applications are authorised by a consultant, shortlists are built by a consultant, and no AI system contacts a candidate or a client on its own. Our data handling is GDPR compliant, and we only use market data sources we have the right to use.

What this means for your hiring

  • Speed. We see hiring activity in your market daily, so we are already talking to the right candidates when a role opens.
  • Quality. Self tagged candidates and a consultant authorisation gate mean the people we present have been matched on accurate data and reviewed by a specialist.
  • Reach. Advert distribution, talent communities, consultant networks and branded advertising combine to reach candidates who are not actively looking.
  • Insight. Salary benchmarks and market intelligence from our own data, available to you throughout an assignment, not only at the pitch.
