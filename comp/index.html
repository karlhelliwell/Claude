Market overview

Compensation, Benefits and HR Technology

The market remains candidate led for highly skilled reward and HR systems specialists as clients continue to invest in talent that can improve transparency, optimise workforce costs, enhance employee experience and deliver technology enabled HR transformation. As a result, we are seeing increasing demand for specialist reward, HR technology, workforce analytics and organisational effectiveness roles. While the broader HR hiring market has been relatively stagnant, organisations continue to compete aggressively for candidates with expertise in executive compensation, pay governance, benefits strategy, pay transparency, Workday, SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM and HR data analytics.

Reward and pay transparency

We are seeing a growing demand for reward professionals who can support pay transparency, pay equity and job architecture initiatives. Employers are preparing for greater scrutiny of reward decisions and responding to increasing employee expectations around fairness and transparency. As a result, candidates with experience in salary benchmarking, building new reward frameworks and grading structures, and remuneration governance are attracting significant interest.

Benefits

Benefits recruitment is also evolving. Organisations are increasingly investing in employee wellbeing, financial wellbeing and flexible benefits programmes as tools for attraction and retention. A recent CIPD survey shows a strong focus on benefits effectiveness, employee engagement, health support and financial wellbeing, creating demand for benefits managers and total reward specialists who can demonstrate measurable return on investment.

HR technology

Within HR technology, demand remains strongest for professionals who can lead HR transformation projects and maximise value from major cloud platforms. There are opportunities for Workday, SAP SuccessFactors and broader HRIS specialists, particularly where organisations are pursuing process automation, AI adoption, workforce analytics and improved reporting capabilities. Budget pressures mean our clients are prioritising candidates who can deliver efficiency improvements and business outcomes rather than purely technical system administration.
