Salary Benchmarking

1

Compare roles with similar responsibilities, experience, and location.

2

Check several current sources, such as salary surveys, job postings, and recruiter insights.

3

Consider the full package, not only base salary, including bonus, benefits, pension or retirement contributions, leave, and contract terms.

4

Decide what range and overall package you would consider before the conversation
