# R logo mark — symbolism

The mark is built from three elements layered onto the letter R:

- **The green arrow** (upward triangle) — *green up*: growth, markets rising.
- **The circle** — *the earth*: the world, grounded reality.
- **The arch** — *god, heaven, beyond the earth*: the aspiration above, crowning the letter as the top of the R.

Read together, bottom to top: growth on earth, reaching toward what is above it.
The arrow and circle also form a figure — a person — at the heart of the letter.
