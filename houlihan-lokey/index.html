The selection process

Example Roadmap

Agile, dedicated approach Execution focused
Week 1

Meeting to define role criteria, specific requirements, and culture.

Week 1

Create a candidate briefing document detailing the role and organisation.

Weeks 2 to 4

Produce a diverse target list of potential candidates.

Weeks 2 to 4

Screen longlist candidates to qualify experience, aspirations and compensation.

Weeks 4 to 7

Present market map. Manage the interview process and provide comprehensive feedback before and after interviews.

Weeks 4 to 7

Formal interviews to evaluate against competencies and culture fit.

Weeks 7 to 10

Optional behavioural and psychometric assessments and final interviews.

Weeks 10 to 13

Manage offer and resignation processes with regular contact.

Beyond

Provide 6 months onboarding support.
